Help — how TurnBook thinks about payouts

Quoted is the clean fee you agreed with the host. Fee is what the marketplace keeps (Turno + payment processing commonly run 10–13% combined on cleaner payouts). Should have paid = quoted − fee. When a payout lands, type what actually arrived into the clean. If it's less than it should be, the Payouts tab shows the variance — screenshot it and ask the platform where the rest went.

Unpaid cleans are tracked separately from variances: a host who hasn't paid at all is a conversation; a platform that paid short is a ticket. TurnBook keeps them apart so you always know which one you're chasing.

Per-clean profit: received − supplies − (miles × your mileage rate). The Jobs tab totals it so you can see which properties actually make money after the drive.
